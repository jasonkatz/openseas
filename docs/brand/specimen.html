The rules in short

Always
  • Solid fills on one shared palette
  • One silhouette a kind, readable at 24 pixels
  • One typeface, sentence case
  • Moves of 120ms, eased, and one beat
  • The character in the words
Never
  • Gradients, gloss, texture, shadow, glow or blur
  • Outlines on a piece, or a second colour in one
  • A frame or grid lines round a board
  • Screen shake, or particles for decoration
  • A colour typed anywhere but the token module
