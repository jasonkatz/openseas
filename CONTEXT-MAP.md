# Context map

## Contexts

- [The puzzle](./docs/puzzles/CONTEXT.md): the pure puzzle, with nothing from the game's world in it
- [The game](./docs/GLOSSARY.md): the space game's words: its world, ships, crews, stations and
  the words they replaced

## Relationships

- **The game → the puzzle**: the game runs rounds of puzzles, dresses each with a skin, wires a
  puzzle's signals to its world, and reads each round's tier and outcome. The puzzle knows nothing of
  the game.
